## Article body

### Opening answer

Answer the main question in two or three sentences. State what the reader can
do, when the procedure is useful, and any important limitation.

### What the task means

Explain the document operation in plain language. Distinguish it from similar
operations when that prevents mistakes.

### Choose the right tool

Describe the relevant tools with descriptive internal links. Explain the result
each one creates instead of merely listing tool names.

### Step-by-step workflow

Use an ordered list. Include file selection, settings, processing, download, and
result verification.

### Practical example

Show one realistic situation and the shortest safe sequence of tools.

### Check the result

Tell the reader what to verify and when to keep the original document.

### Frequently asked questions

Include two to four questions answered directly in the article.

### Relevant call to action

Link to the most relevant category page or tool using descriptive anchor text.

## Pre-publication check

- Only one H1 is visible.
- Heading order is logical.
- Every internal link opens the intended public page.
- SEO title and meta description are unique.
- The article is readable on mobile.
- No paragraph or heading exists only to repeat a keyword.
- Claims about privacy, security, or legal validity are appropriately qualified.
